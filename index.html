BLOCK BOARD: RIVALS

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Block Board: Rivals, a mobile puzzle game developed and published by HALA Studio.

HALA Studio respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information and providing transparency about how information is collected, processed, stored, and used when you access our game and its associated online services.

This Privacy Policy explains our data practices, the purposes for which information is processed, the third-party services we may use, and the privacy rights and choices available to you.

This Privacy Policy applies to Block Board: Rivals on supported mobile platforms, including Android and iOS, and to its associated online services.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Guest Player Accounts

Block Board: Rivals may automatically create a guest player account when you first launch the application or access its online services.

You are not required to provide your real name, email address, phone number, or other directly identifying contact information to access standard guest gameplay.

Information associated with a guest account may include:

  • Unique player account identifiers
  • Application installation identifiers
  • Authentication and session information
  • Automatically generated or selected usernames
  • Selected profile avatars
  • Player levels and experience points
  • Account creation and last activity timestamps
  • Account preferences and settings

Although guest accounts do not require your real name or contact details, account identifiers and associated information may constitute personal data under applicable privacy laws.

2.2. Optional Account Linking

HALA Studio plans to introduce optional account linking through supported third-party authentication providers, including Google and Apple.

Once available, this functionality may allow players to link an existing guest account to a supported authentication provider to preserve and recover their gameplay progress across supported devices.

Depending on the authentication provider and permissions granted, information received may include:

  • A unique authentication provider identifier
  • Email address, where provided
  • Account display name, where provided
  • Authentication and account linking information

HALA Studio does not receive or store your Google or Apple account password through these authentication services.

Account linking is intended to remain optional. Information associated with these services will only be processed when the relevant functionality becomes available and is used by the player.

2.3. Gameplay Information

We may collect, process, and store gameplay information necessary to operate the game and its associated online features.

This information may include:

  • Game scores and personal records
  • Gameplay progress and statistics
  • Player levels and experience points
  • Achievements and unlocked content
  • Online multiplayer match results
  • Wins, losses, and competitive rankings
  • Leaderboard positions
  • World Arenas participation and results
  • Tournament participation and results, where available
  • Gameplay actions and move history
  • Selected themes and cosmetic customisations

Gameplay information may be stored locally on your device or processed by our backend services, depending on the feature being used.

2.4. Virtual Currency and Rewards

Block Board: Rivals includes virtual gameplay currencies, rewards, and digital items.

Information associated with these features may include:

  • B-Coin balances
  • Virtual currency transaction history
  • Competitive arena entry fees and rewards
  • Tournament ticket balances, where available
  • Daily rewards and redemption records
  • Lucky Spin participation and reward history
  • Rewarded advertisement completion records
  • Virtual item purchases using in-game currency
  • Unlocked cosmetic items and customisations
Virtual currency notice

B-Coins, tournament tickets, Lucky Spin rewards, and other virtual items have no real-world monetary value.

Block Board: Rivals does not currently offer real-money in-app purchases. B-Coins cannot be purchased with real money, withdrawn, exchanged for real-world currency, or redeemed for real-world monetary prizes through our services.

When you access our online services, technical information may be processed to establish connections, maintain service reliability, authenticate accounts, and protect the game against abuse.

Depending on the services used, this information may include:

  • IP addresses
  • Application version information

  • Error and diagnostic information
  • Security-related activity

2.6. Support Communications

If you contact HALA Studio for technical support, feedback, account assistance, or privacy-related requests, we may process the information you voluntarily provide.

This may include your email address, the contents of your communication, relevant player account identifiers, screenshots, and information necessary to investigate and respond to your request.

11.1. Inactive Guest Accounts

Planned guest account retention policy

HALA Studio plans to introduce automatic deletion of unlinked guest accounts after 90 consecutive days of inactivity.

Once this functionality is introduced, inactivity will be measured from the most recent authenticated activity associated with the player account.

Unlinked guest accounts that remain inactive for at least 90 consecutive days may be automatically and permanently deleted during scheduled account maintenance.

Automatic deletion may permanently remove:

  • The player account and profile
  • Authentication and session information
  • Gameplay progress and statistics
  • Personal records and leaderboard entries
  • B-Coin balances and associated records
  • Tournament tickets and virtual rewards
  • Lucky Spin records
  • Unlocked cosmetic items and achievements
  • Other information associated with the account

Once optional account linking becomes available, accounts linked to a supported Google or Apple authentication service will not be subject to this automatic guest account inactivity deletion policy.

Linked accounts will remain subject to other applicable data retention, account deletion, security, and legal requirements.

Certain historical information may be retained in anonymised form where it is necessary to preserve competitive records, aggregate statistics, or the match histories of other players.

Information required for legitimate security, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or legal purposes may be retained where necessary and permitted by applicable law.

Once a guest account has been permanently deleted, its associated progress, virtual currency, rewards, and digital items cannot be recovered.
